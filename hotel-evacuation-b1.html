1. Emergency Enquiries & Exit Locations

  • "Could you tell me where the emergency exit staircase is located?"
  • "Which corridor leads directly to the ground floor assembly point?"
  • "Is the main staircase blocked by smoke?"
  • "Could you confirm if the fire department has been notified?"
  • "Where is the emergency evacuation plan posted on this floor?"

2. Evacuation Routes & Prepositions of Movement

  • "Go straight down the hall and turn right past the elevator."
  • "Head through the fire doors and follow the green escape signs."
  • "Do not use the main elevator during a fire emergency."
  • "Take the service stairs down to the basement exit."
  • "Walk carefully along the wall towards the safety door."

3. Urgent Assistance & Safety Requests

  • "Is it okay if we pass through the service kitchen exit?"
  • "We have to reach the outdoor assembly area immediately!"
  • "Could you please help us open this fire blanket cabinet?"
  • "I need to pick up the emergency master keycard from the box."
  • "Please hurry, smoke is filling the hallway!"

4. Incident Reporting & Safety Declarations

  • "Excuse me, the emergency exit door isn't opening."
  • "Could you tell me if the alarm has already alerted security?"
  • "We do not have any missing guests in our group."
  • "Is the service corridor clear of hazardous obstacles?"
  • "There is heavy smoke blocking the 4th-floor landing."
