Essential B2 Airport Vocabulary

  • Tight connection: A very short time window between connecting flights.
  • Inter-terminal transit: Moving between separated airport terminal buildings.
  • Expedite: To speed up a process or movement.
  • Airside / Landside: Beyond security (airside) vs. public airport area (landside).
  • Customs declaration: Official statement regarding carried goods and currency.
  • Boarding priority: Right to board before general passengers.

Direct & Professional Expressions

  • • "Hi, I have a tight connection for flight AP-88—can I buy an express shuttle ticket here?"
  • • "Excuse me officer, my flight leaves in 20 minutes. Is there a fast-track lane I can use?"
  • • "Laptop and power bank are in separate trays. Does the tablet need to come out as well?"
  • • "Nothing to declare, officer—just standard personal belongings."
  • • "I appreciate your situation, but I specifically booked an aisle seat for a back condition."
