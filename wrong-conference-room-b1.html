1. Agenda Enquiries & Room Locations

  • "Excuse me, could you tell me which building section the keynote meeting is in?"
  • "Could you tell me if the keynote presentation has already started?"
  • "Which corridor leads directly to Auditorium 8?"
  • "Is there a skybridge connecting the North and South wings?"
  • "What time do security barriers lock down Sector A?"

2. Complex Directions & Prepositions of Movement

  • "Go straight across the skybridge and turn right past the information kiosk."
  • "Head down the central stairwell toward the South Wing concourse."
  • "Walk carefully along the marked carpet through the exhibition hall."
  • "Which glass door leads to the executive conference room?"
  • "Follow the overhead signs through the VIP service passage."

3. Urgent Requests & Modals of Necessity

  • "We have to reach Auditorium 8 before the keynote starts."
  • "Is it okay if we pass through this service corridor now?"
  • "Could you please help us scan our conference badges?"
  • "I would like to pick up my presentation folder from locker 9."
  • "Please hurry, security is restricting building access!"

4. Malfunction Reporting & Formal Statements

  • "Excuse me, my conference pass isn't working right now."
  • "Could you tell me if the registration gate has already closed?"
  • "We do not have any unauthorized recording gear in our possession."
  • "Is the main conference hall clear of technical maintenance?"
  • "There is an electronic badge scanner error at checkpoint 2."
