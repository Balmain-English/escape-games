1. Disembarkation & Departure Enquiries

  • "Is it okay if I return to Deck 5 to collect my forgotten bag?"
  • "Could you tell me if the disembarkation ramp has already closed?"
  • "What time does the ferry depart for the next port?"
  • "Could you confirm if the gangway is still open for passengers?"
  • "Which exit deck leads directly to the car terminal?"

2. Onboard Directions & Deck Layout

  • "Go straight up the stairs and turn left past the lounge."
  • "Head through the service doors to reach the lower car deck."
  • "Take the main stairwell down to the disembarkation exit."
  • "Which corridor leads to the observation lounge on Deck 5?"
  • "Walk past the duty-free shop toward the central elevator."

3. Urgent Requests & Onboard Retrieval

  • "I need to pick up my laptop bag from under seat 14."
  • "We have to reach the exit deck before the gangway closes!"
  • "Could you please hold the door open for us?"
  • "Please hurry, the ferry is about to pull away from the dock!"
  • "Could you please help us locate the lost property kiosk?"

4. Incident Reporting & Security Clearances

  • "Excuse me, the automatic fire door isn't opening."
  • "Could you tell me if anyone has turned in a black laptop bag?"
  • "I do not have any restricted items in my luggage."
  • "Is the main passenger staircase open for disembarkation?"
  • "There is an obstacle blocking the service passage on Deck 3."
