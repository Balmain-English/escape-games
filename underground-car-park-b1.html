1. Facility Enquiries & Level Directions

  • "Excuse me, could you tell me which level customer parking is on?"
  • "Could you tell me how to get to Zone B from this elevator lobby?"
  • "Which corridor leads directly to the automated payment machines?"
  • "What time do the underground exit shutters lock for the night?"
  • "Is there a customer service desk open on Level -2?"

2. Machine Validation & Payment Queries

  • "Could you tell me if this payment station accepts credit cards?"
  • "Excuse me, the payment machine isn't accepting my card."
  • "Could you please check this extra charge on my parking stub?"
  • "I would like to validate my parking ticket before driving to the barrier."
  • "Where can I scan my cinema ticket stub for discounted parking?"

3. Urgent Requests & Modals of Obligation

  • "We have to reach the exit barrier before the security shutters close!"
  • "Is it okay if we drive through this service ramp to reach the exit?"
  • "I would like to pick up my replacement validated ticket, please."
  • "Please hurry, the automatic shutters are closing in five minutes!"
  • "I need to clear the exit gate before lockdown."

4. Incident Reporting & Security Clearances

  • "Excuse me, the automatic barrier arm isn't lifting."
  • "We do not have any unpaid fees on our parking ticket."
  • "Could you tell me if the exit shutter has already been locked?"
  • "Is the express exit lane clear of maintenance obstacles?"
  • "There is an electronic scanner error at barrier gate 2."
