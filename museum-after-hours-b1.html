1. Technical Enquiries & Authorized Exits

  • "Excuse me, could you tell me if there is a technical problem with the main doors?"
  • "Could you tell me if the staff exit in Sector C is still unlocked?"
  • "Which corridor leads directly to the authorized exit courtyard?"
  • "Is there a secondary escape route open past the exhibition hall?"
  • "What time does the automatic security grid lock for the night?"

2. Gallery Directions & Floor Plan Prepositions

  • "Go straight through the archway and turn left past the marble statues."
  • "Head down the central stairwell toward the lower conservation lab."
  • "Walk carefully along the marked walkway through the gallery."
  • "Which door leads to the curatorial storage area on Level 1?"
  • "Follow the emergency signs through the staff service passageway."

3. Urgent Requests & Modals of Obligation

  • "We have to reach the staff corridor before the central system shuts down."
  • "Is it okay if we pass through this service corridor now?"
  • "Could you please help us open this emergency security door?"
  • "I would like to pick up my coat from the staff locker."
  • "Please hurry, the automatic glass barriers are closing!"

4. Incident Reporting & Security Clearances

  • "Excuse me, the automatic barrier isn't responding."
  • "Could you tell me if the authorized exit gate has already been locked?"
  • "We do not have any museum artifacts in our possession."
  • "Is the restoration passageway clear of technical obstacles?"
  • "There is an electronic door failure in Sector A."
