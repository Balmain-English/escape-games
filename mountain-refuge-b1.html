1. Weather Forecasts & Condition Enquiries

  • "Could you tell me which route is safest before the blizzard arrives?"
  • "Is the lower mountain pass still open for hikers?"
  • "What time is the snowstorm expected to reach the peak?"
  • "Could you confirm if the weather radio is receiving clear signals?"
  • "Which trail avoids the steep cliff path during bad weather?"

2. Hiking Directions & Prepositions of Movement

  • "Go straight down the trail and turn left past the rocky ridge."
  • "Head through the forest path to stay below the tree line."
  • "Walk carefully along the marked yellow safety ropes."
  • "Which trail leads directly to the valley rescue shelter?"
  • "Take the wooden bridge across the river gorge."

3. Urgent Safety Requests & Modals

  • "We have to descend below the tree line before visibility drops."
  • "Is it okay if we wait inside the shelter until the bus arrives?"
  • "Could you please help us secure this safety rope?"
  • "I need to pick up my winter jacket from locker 8."
  • "Please hurry, the blizzard is approaching fast!"

4. Incident Reporting & Rescue Declarations

  • "Excuse me, the emergency radio isn't working right now."
  • "Could you tell me if the mountain pass has already been closed?"
  • "We do not have any injured hikers in our group."
  • "Is the lower valley road clear of snow blockages?"
  • "There is heavy fog covering the upper mountain ridge."
