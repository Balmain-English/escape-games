1. Malfunction Reports & Exit Enquiries

  • "Excuse me, could you tell me where the emergency stairs are located?"
  • "Could you confirm if the reception lobby is on the ground floor?"
  • "Which corridor leads directly to the fire exit door?"
  • "Is there a service elevator working in this wing?"
  • "What time does the main reception desk close?"

2. Hallway Directions & Prepositions of Movement

  • "Go straight down the hallway and turn right past the ice machine."
  • "Head through the heavy fire doors toward the stairwell."
  • "Walk down the carpeted corridor to reach the west wing."
  • "Which door leads to the staff maintenance staircase?"
  • "Follow the green exit signs through the service hallway."

3. Urgent Requests & Modals of Necessity

  • "We have to reach reception before our appointment starts!"
  • "Is it okay if we use this service door right now?"
  • "Could you please help us open this heavy stairwell door?"
  • "I would like to pick up my luggage from the storage room."
  • "Please hurry, our transfer driver is waiting in the lobby!"

4. Incident Reporting & Formal Statements

  • "Excuse me, the main passenger elevator isn't working."
  • "Could you tell me if our airport transfer has already arrived?"
  • "We do not have any hotel property in our possession."
  • "Is the main staircase clear of cleaning equipment?"
  • "There is an electronic door failure on Floor 14."
