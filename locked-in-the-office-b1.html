1. Security Protocols & Access Control

  • "Could you tell me where the emergency exit keycard is kept?"
  • "I need an emergency access badge to open this sector."
  • "Is this exit clear or is the automatic alarm activated?"
  • "We need to evacuate before the security system locks down."
  • "Please authorize our emergency clearance code immediately."

2. Office Directions & Locations

  • "Head past the conference room and turn right."
  • "The emergency staircase is located at the end of the hall."
  • "Which floor leads directly to the main exit lobby?"
  • "Take the emergency stairwell down to the basement dock."
  • "Go through the double glass doors past the reception desk."

3. Urgent Workplace Requests & Modals

  • "We have to leave the building before the doors lock completely."
  • "Could you please help us open this maintenance hatch?"
  • "We must find the exit keycard as quickly as possible."
  • "Should we check the IT room for the master key?"
  • "Please hurry, the security system locks down in minutes!"

4. Tech & Facilities Reporting

  • "Excuse me, the terminal card reader isn't working right now."
  • "There is an automatic lockdown alert on floor 4."
  • "I think there is a malfunction in the digital keypad."
  • "Please turn off the emergency security shutter."
  • "We do not have any restricted equipment in our possession."
