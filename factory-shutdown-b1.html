1. Shift Shutdowns & Exit Enquiries

  • "Could you tell me where the emergency exit door is located?"
  • "What time does the perimeter security gate lock automatically?"
  • "Is there a secondary exit open near Sector B?"
  • "Could you confirm if all assembly lines have stopped?"
  • "Which door leads directly to the outdoor assembly area?"

2. Factory Directions & Prepositions

  • "Go straight through the corridor and turn left past the hazard sign."
  • "Head down the gangway toward the lower maintenance bay."
  • "Walk carefully along the marked yellow safety line."
  • "Which corridor leads to the central control room?"
  • "Take the metal stairs down to the ground floor exit."

3. Urgent Requests & Modals of Obligation

  • "We have to reach Sector B before the perimeter doors lock!"
  • "Is it okay if we pass through this service corridor now?"
  • "Could you please help us open this safety equipment locker?"
  • "I need to pick up my safety helmet from locker 4."
  • "Please hurry, the automatic shutters are closing!"

4. Incident Reporting & Safety Declarations

  • "Excuse me, the automatic safety shutter isn't responding."
  • "Could you tell me if the perimeter gate has already been locked?"
  • "We do not have any hazardous materials in our possession."
  • "Is the service passage clear of heavy machinery?"
  • "There is an emergency lockout alert in Sector A."
