1. Safety Procedures & Shutdown Enquiries

  • "Could you tell me where the emergency shutdown procedure guide is?"
  • "Could you confirm if the ventilation system is still operating?"
  • "Which corridor leads directly to the authorized exit airlock?"
  • "Is there a safety bypass switch open in Sector B?"
  • "What time do the automatic containment doors lock down?"

2. Lab Directions & Prepositions of Movement

  • "Go straight through the containment zone and turn right past the safety shower."
  • "Head down the central service corridor toward the decontamination airlock."
  • "Walk carefully along the marked yellow safety pathway."
  • "Which glass door leads to the cold sample vault?"
  • "Follow the green exit indicators through the bio-safety passage."

3. Urgent Requests & Modals of Necessity

  • "We have to clear the main corridor before the airlock seals!"
  • "Is it okay if we pass through this service passage now?"
  • "Could you please help us secure this chemical storage locker?"
  • "I need to pick up my research samples from freezer 9."
  • "Please hurry, the automatic seal is closing in five minutes!"

4. Malfunction Reporting & Formal Statements

  • "Excuse me, the automatic airlock door isn't working right now."
  • "Could you tell me if the authorized exit door has already locked?"
  • "We do not have any hazardous materials in our possession."
  • "Is the main exhaust system clear of technical obstructions?"
  • "There is an electronic door sensor failure in Sector A."
