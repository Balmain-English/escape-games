1. Line Transfers & Station Closures

  • "Could you tell me which connecting line is still running?"
  • "What time does the last train on Line 4 depart?"
  • "Is there a service disruption on the blue line?"
  • "Could you confirm if the station entrance has already closed?"
  • "Which platform connects directly to the surface exit?"

2. Underground Navigation & Directions

  • "Go straight through the tunnel and turn right past the ticket machine."
  • "Head down the stairs to reach the lower transfer platform."
  • "Which passageway leads to the emergency street exit?"
  • "Walk past the information booth toward Line 2."
  • "Take the escalator up to the central concourse level."

3. Urgent Requests & Modal Obligation

  • "We have to reach Platform 2 before the last train departs!"
  • "Is it okay if we pass through this service gate now?"
  • "Could you please help us open this ticket barrier?"
  • "I need to pick up my wallet from the lost property box."
  • "Please hurry, the metro doors are about to close!"

4. Incident Reporting & Gate Rules

  • "Excuse me, my metro pass isn't opening the gate."
  • "Could you tell me if the last train has already left?"
  • "We do not have any restricted equipment in our possession."
  • "Is the main transfer corridor clear of maintenance work?"
  • "There is an automatic gate error at entrance B."
