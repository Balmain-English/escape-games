1. Flight Status & Terminal Announcements

  • "Excuse me, could you tell me if flight AP-404 departs from Terminal 2?"
  • "Which terminal handles international transfers?"
  • "Is there a gate change announced for the London flight?"
  • "Could you confirm if final boarding has already started?"
  • "What time does boarding close for gate B24?"

2. Airport Navigation & Gate Directions

  • "Go straight past the duty-free shop and turn right toward Gate B24."
  • "Which concourse leads to the shuttle platform?"
  • "Follow the overhead signs to reach the passport clearance desk."
  • "Head past the information kiosk and take the moving walkway."
  • "Take the escalator up to the upper departures lounge."

3. Urgent Security & Baggage Requests

  • "Is it okay if I use the fast-track security lane?"
  • "We have to reach Gate B24 before boarding closes!"
  • "I would like to pick up my missing pouch from Lost & Found."
  • "Could you please hurry? My flight leaves in 15 minutes!"
  • "Must I remove my belt for this security scanner?"

4. Boarding Enquiries & Gate Procedures

  • "Could you tell me if Gate B24 is currently boarding?"
  • "What time does the next terminal shuttle leave?"
  • "I don't have anything illegal to declare."
  • "Is this line reserved for priority boarding passengers?"
  • "Where can I scan my electronic boarding pass?"
