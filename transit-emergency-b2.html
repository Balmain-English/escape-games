Essential B2 Airport Vocabulary

  • Tight connection: A very short time window between connecting flights.
  • Inter-terminal transit: Moving between separated airport terminal buildings.
  • Expedite: To speed up a process or movement.
  • Airside / Landside: Beyond security (airside) vs. public airport area (landside).
  • Customs declaration: Official statement regarding carried goods and currency.
  • Boarding priority: Right to board before general passengers.
  • Accommodate: To adjust or help meet someone's request.

Formal, Polite & Direct Expressions

  • • "Could you kindly direct me to the expedited transit lane?"
  • • "I am currently on a very tight connection for flight AP-88."
  • • "Would it be possible to utilize the fast-track security lane?"
  • • "I wish to declare personal effects within standard allowances."
  • • "I completely understand your request; however, I must respectfully decline."
  • • "I would be more than happy to accommodate your seat request."
