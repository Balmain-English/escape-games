1. Bills & Financial Requests

  • "Excuse me, I think there is a mistake on my bill."
  • "Could you please check this extra charge again?"
  • "I didn't order this item; could you recalculate the total?"
  • "Could I get an itemized receipt for my expenses?"
  • "Is service included in the final bill?"

2. Transit, Schedules & Connections

  • "Which line should I take to get to the airport link?"
  • "What time does the next airport shuttle depart?"
  • "Is this a direct train or do I need to transfer?"
  • "Could you tell me which platform the express departs from?"
  • "Is the shuttle running on schedule right now?"

3. Urgent Requests & Home Imperatives

  • "I have an urgent home emergency and must leave A.S.A.P."
  • "Could you please take me to the airport as fast as possible?"
  • "I would like to pick up my stored luggage immediately."
  • "I have to catch the very next flight home."
  • "Please hurry, every minute counts for my connection!"

4. Airport, Upgrades & Security Clearances

  • "Since economy is fully booked, are there Business Class seats?"
  • "I need to upgrade my ticket to board tonight's flight."
  • "Is it okay if I take this small bag as carry-on?"
  • "I don't have anything illegal to declare."
  • "Could you tell me if boarding has already started?"
