1. Schedules & Platform Announcements

  • "Could you tell me which platform the express train leaves from?"
  • "What time does the last train to Central Station depart?"
  • "Is there a platform change for the 23:00 departure?"
  • "Please announce if the express service is running on time."
  • "Which track is reserved for the night express connection?"

2. Station Directions & Detours

  • "Go straight through the tunnel and turn left past the cafe."
  • "Which corridor leads directly to Platform 5?"
  • "Is the main stairwell closed for renovation?"
  • "Follow the emergency signs to reach the lower platform level."
  • "Head past the information desk and take the escalator down."

3. Urgent Transit Requests & Modals

  • "We have to take the stairs because the train leaves in 8 minutes!"
  • "Could you please help us locate Coach B on this platform?"
  • "I must catch this connection or I will be stranded tonight."
  • "Should we hurry to the rear carriage before doors lock?"
  • "Please open the ticket gate, every second counts!"

4. Tickets, Gates & Machine Errors

  • "Excuse me, the ticket validator isn't accepting my pass."
  • "Is it okay if I pass through this gate with an e-ticket?"
  • "I need to pick up my luggage from locker 12 immediately."
  • "Could you tell me if the train doors have already closed?"
  • "Where can I validate my paper ticket for Platform 5?"
